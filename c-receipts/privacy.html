C Receipts Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 8, 2026

C Receipts is an internal expense-submission app published by Confluence, Inc. ("Confluence", "we", "us") for use by Confluence employees and contractors. This policy explains what the app collects, where that information goes, how long it is kept, and how to have it deleted.

What the app collects

Your account identity. When you sign in, the app reads your name and email address from your Microsoft account. It does this so the app can show who is signed in and send receipts from your own mailbox.

Receipt images. Images you capture with the camera or select from your photo library. The app crops and straightens them on your device before sending.

Purchase details you enter. The reason for the purchase and the receipt total.

That is the complete list. The app does not collect location, contacts, calendar, health data, advertising identifiers, or any behavioral or usage analytics. There is no advertising, no tracking, and no profiling. We do not sell or rent personal information to anyone.

How the information is used

The sole purpose is expense submission. When you tap Send Receipt, the app sends an email from your own Confluence mailbox to receipts@thinkconfluence.com. That email contains the receipt image as an attachment, along with the reason and amount you entered.

Confluence does not operate any separate server that receives your receipts. The only destination is the corporate mailbox above, inside Confluence's own Microsoft 365 environment.

What is stored on your device

Sign-in credentials. A secure sign-in token is stored in your device's protected credential store and can only be used on that device while it is unlocked. Your password is never seen by, or stored in, the app. You sign in through Microsoft's own login page in your device's browser.

Recent receipt history. The app keeps a local record of your most recent 50 submissions: the reason, the amount, the date, and a small thumbnail of the receipt. This is a convenience feature and stays on your device. It is never uploaded anywhere.

Third parties

The app shares information with the following parties only, and only as described. Each provides protection of your data equal to or greater than that described in this policy.

Microsoft. Handles sign-in, provides your name and email address, and sends, delivers, and stores the receipt emails. This takes place inside Confluence's own Microsoft 365 environment and is governed by Microsoft's standard data protection terms.

Expo. The app uses Expo's update service to deliver improvements without a full app reinstall. When checking for updates, the app sends technical details such as the app version and your device platform. No receipt images, purchase details, account identity, or credentials are sent to Expo.

Apple. Distributes the app and associates the installation with your Apple Account. Apple receives no receipt data from the app.

Retention and deletion

Receipt history on your device. Capped at the 50 most recent submissions. Older entries are discarded automatically. You can delete all of it at any time using Clear History in the account panel. It is also cleared automatically when you sign out, and removed entirely if you delete the app.

Sign-in credentials on your device. Removed when you sign out and when you delete the app.

Submitted receipts. Once sent, a receipt is an ordinary email in Confluence's mailboxes, in both your Sent Items and the receipts@thinkconfluence.com inbox. It is retained under Confluence's standard business records retention schedule, which may be longer than the retention of on-device data because these are financial records. To request deletion or correction of a submitted receipt, contact privacy@thinkconfluence.com.

Withdrawing consent

You can stop the app from accessing your information at any time by any of the following:

  • Sign out in the app, which clears your stored credentials and local receipt history.
  • Revoke the app's access to your Microsoft account at myaccount.microsoft.com.
  • Revoke camera or photo library permission in your device's Settings app. The app will continue to run, but capture will be unavailable.
  • Delete the app, which removes all locally stored data.

Withdrawing consent does not retroactively delete receipts already emailed. Use the contact address above for those.

Children

The app is an internal workplace tool. It is not directed to children and is not intended for anyone under 16.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top and publish the revised version at the same URL. Material changes will be communicated internally.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: privacy@thinkconfluence.com
